# Techno Bowl — digital

A browser version of the TECHNO BOWL tabletop football game (Bombshell Games / More Fun! League),
built from the owner's purchased print-and-play files. Structure and look follow **Long Shot** (the quality bar,
esp. cameras and cinematics) and **Escape Order 66** (sound editor, kits), both siblings on the Desktop.

- Repo: github.com/anthonypatryn/tech-no-bowl (private). Vercel deploys on push to `main`. The site is open (no password gate).
- Plan and batches: `docs/PLAN.md`. Rules and all 32 rosters (transcribed): `docs/rules-and-rosters.md`.

## Board geometry
- 1 world unit = 1 square = 5 yards. The play area is **13 columns × 24 rows**, end zones included (2 rows each end).
  It's centred on the origin: columns run along x, rows along z.
- Row 0 is the blue end zone (−z), row 23 the orange one. The broadcast camera sits on the +x sideline.
- Field image calibration (inner sideline and end-line edges) is in `art.json` → `field`. Check it on Art Review → Field.

## Engine (Techno mode)
- Request types a coach answers:
  - Setup: `toss`, `formation`, `call` (5 cards), `motion`, `timeout`.
  - Cards: `pick` (next card from the bench), `readReact`, `first` (who acts first).
  - Activation: `act` (step by step: move / stand / handoff / block / tackle / pass / adjust / end), `bonus` (a free
    full activation or half move), `adjustPartner`.
  - Checks and contact: `boost` (bench boost), `reverse`, `push`, `followUp`, `scatter`.
- Each `act` request lists every legal choice (`moves` with costs, `passes` with modifiers, `blocks`, `tackles`,
  `handoffs`), so the UI only highlights what the engine offers.
- Movement inside an activation is a series of moves. The diagonal count carries across them. Block, tackle and pass
  need spent ≤ half the move (read-and-react: nothing spent yet).
- A play ends by throwing `PlayEnd` (td / safety / tackle / out / incomplete / stall). `endPlay` handles score, downs,
  turnovers on downs and the clock.
- Log entries carry data (pid, from/to/path, dice, result). B2 animates from them.

### Rulings (things the rulebook leaves open; change here if the owner disagrees)
- Benches are dealt fresh every down (2 cards per player on the field).
- A ball carrier knocked down by anything except a tackle fumbles (scatter). A dramatic recovery (scatter tie) by a
  carrier who is down ends the down like a tackle.
- A scatter that lands on a standing player: he gets an automatic sport check to grab it. Fail, or a knocked-down
  player: it scatters again.
- Pass penalty = opponents threatening the target plus opponents threatening the passer, capped at 3.
- After a turnover only lateral or backward passes are allowed. A carrier pushed into the opponent's end zone scores.
- Defensive pressure triggers once per defender activation, when he moves from outside the carrier's threat zone to
  inside it (never after he has stepped out of it in the same activation).
- Teams don't switch ends at half time (side 0 always attacks row 0).
- Not implemented yet: play clocks (off by default anyway), PRO/ALL PRO/INFERNO, skills, field goals.

## Art pipeline
- Every uniform sheet is 8 cube nets. The middle square is the top (number on team colour). The 4 arms are the
  player standing, head toward the middle, rotated upright by the script.
- The sides are the same sprite (only its placement on the turf varies), so atlases keep just **top + front**
  (`cols: 2`), one row per player in ascending jersey order.
- The team comes from the title printed on each sheet, so a misnamed file still lands on the right team. If two sheets
  for a team have identical art, only the first is kept: `Rattlers_RattlerRed` and `Rattlers_RedRattler` are the same,
  so "Rattler Red" is kept. (The owner confirmed that sheet is Rattlers; it was once misnamed `Reapers_RattlerRed`.)
- The down-marker sheet keeps all 5 faces. Row 6 is the 1/2/3/4 cube.
- Turf is the middle square of a plain grass net. Field: 4K and 2K WebP.
- Re-run with `npm run art` (needs Python + PyMuPDF, OpenCV, Pillow). The source art must sit in the project root.
